# Poloko IKS — Image Generation Brief
## For AI Image Generation / Photography Direction

**Project:** Poloko IKS — Indigenous Knowledge Vault & Research Innovation System
**Context:** Open-source heritage preservation platform built for Botswana
**Aesthetic:** Warm, editorial, heritage-grade. Earth tones — deep terracotta (`#8B2500`), amber, sage green, parchment. Inspired by Botswana's Kalahari landscape, Okavango Delta, and traditional Tswana craft patterns.
**Avoid:** Stock photography clichés, poverty narratives, colonial framing, white saviour aesthetics, overly saturated HDR. No generic "Africa" imagery.

---

## Global Style Guide for All Images

| Property | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Colour temperature** | Warm — golden hour, midday Botswana sun, amber light |
| **Palette** | Terracotta, ochre, burnt sienna, sage green, natural parchment |
| **Texture** | Organic — dry earth, weathered bark, woven baskets, hand-written text |
| **People** | Dignified, active, not posed. Real communities, not models. Diversity of age including elders and youth. |
| **Aspect ratios** | See per-image spec below |
| **File format requested** | PNG or WEBP, minimum 1600px on the longest edge |

## IMAGE 01 — Landing Page Hero Background

**Placement:** Full-bleed hero section background (behind the terracotta gradient overlay)
**File name:** `hero-background.webp`
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9 or wider (1920×1080 minimum)
**Usage:** The image sits beneath a `linear-gradient(150deg, #5C1006 0%, #8B2500 35%, #B84010 65%, #C87820 100%)` overlay at ~65% opacity. The image shows through at the edges and in subtle texture.

**Prompt:**
> Aerial or wide-angle photograph of the Botswana landscape at golden hour — dry savanna earth, sparse acacia trees, and a distant village silhouette. The light is warm amber-orange, casting long shadows across cracked clay soil. No people visible — pure landscape. The horizon is wide and open. Colours: deep ochre, burnt sienna, dusty terracotta, thin line of sage-green bush. High resolution, documentary photography style, no filters.

**Alternative:** A close-up macro of traditional Tswana woven basket (tswana bask/lekupa) in deep amber and natural grass colours, slightly out of focus at edges, used as a texture layer.

## Recommended AI Image Tools

- **Midjourney** — best for landscape photography realism and warm editorial tones
- **Adobe Firefly** — good for culturally sensitive content with reference image upload
- **DALL·E 3** — good for flat-lay product and macro shots
- **Stable Diffusion (SDXL)** — useful for texture and pattern generation (images 07, 18)

**Reference artists / styles to cite in prompts:**
- *Brent Stirton* — documentary Africa photography (dignified, editorial)
- *George Steinmetz* — Botswana aerial landscape (images 01, 04, 12)
- *Zanele Muholi* — portraiture with dignity (images 05, 11, 14)
